What I build

Setups for the work that eats the week.

Each setup is built for your business, runs in its own private account, and comes with a written guide. You approve anything before it goes out.

Reception agent

Answers website chat, texts, and email: hours, what you sell, directions, and basic questions about what you charge. It takes down the customer's name and what they need, books them on your Google Calendar, and texts you when a person should step in. A phone version is available.

Lead finder and outreach

Watches the public listings that matter to you, like estate sales, permits, new business filings, or job posts, and picks out the good matches. It drafts one honest email per match. You approve it, and replies come to you as leads.

Back-office automation

Form to spreadsheet to invoice or contract. New client intake to a folder and checklist. A weekly numbers report. Built on Google Workspace.

Custom builds

For work that doesn't fit a setup: a custom dashboard, a tool for your own process, or several setups working together. I scope each one with you first.

Each setup includes ongoing care, quoted with the setup.

My own tools

What I'm building

Alongside client work, I build my own tools. Ostium, Maestro, and Mnemos are in the works; I'll open source them once they work as intended.

Ostium

Working name

One place to run and check work from several AI coding assistants at once. A meta harness. It now covers much of what Surfaces used to do.

Maestro

Picks the right AI model for each job, so simple work goes to fast, cheaper models and the expensive ones only get used when they're needed.

Mnemos

Memory that stays on your own computer, so your AI tools remember past work without handing it to someone else's cloud.
