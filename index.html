The problem

Most businesses rent their AI. That gets expensive.

The bill grows with use

Every chat, agent and automation is metered by the token. The more you rely on it, the more you pay, and someone else sets the price.

Your edge leaks out

Customer lists, pricing, the way you actually do the work. Send it to the wrong tool and you're training the next model, and your competitors, on it.

You're locked in

Build everything around one vendor and you inherit their decisions: price changes, model swaps, outages, features that disappear.

Nobody on your team knows how it works

When the consultant leaves, the automation turns into a black box. Nobody can fix it, and nobody trusts it.

None of this means "don't use AI." It means use it on terms that leave you better off.

Why local, now

Owning your AI now makes sense for small businesses

Open models got good and cheap

Labs in China and the US keep releasing strong open-weight models that anyone can download. That pushes prices down and makes running AI yourself realistic on ordinary hardware.

The big labs ship every few weeks

Great for capability. Hard to build a stable business on if everything is tied to one vendor's latest release.

Usage keeps climbing

Agents and automations use far more tokens than chat. A bill that's small today grows quietly as you rely on it more.

“Free” AI isn't free

Free-token products are paid for with your data and your workflows. That's the part worth protecting.

My bet: the businesses that do well will use cloud AI where it shines and own the rest. I help you find that line.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need my own hardware to work with you?

No. Most clients start with automations on tools they already use. Local is an option, not a requirement.

What does "local" actually mean?

The AI model runs on a computer you own, in your office or on your network, so the data it handles doesn't leave.

Is local AI as good as ChatGPT or Claude?

Not for everything. For a lot of everyday business work, like sorting, drafting, summarizing and answering routine questions, open models are now good enough. I'll tell you where they fall short and use a frontier model there.

Which AI tools do you recommend?

It depends on your work and what's sensitive. That's part of the free call and the written plan. I don't take vendor commissions, so the advice is just the advice.

Will the AI send things without me?

Not unless you want it to. By default you approve anything before it goes out.

What happens if we stop working together?

You keep everything: code, accounts, data and the guide. It keeps running.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the job. The call is free, and I give a fixed quote before any build.

Who is this for?

Small and mid-size businesses with real manual work: local shops, service businesses, small teams.
