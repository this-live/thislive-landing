Questions and answers

Before you book

What happens on the triage call?

It is a 30-minute video call. We look at the workflows that cost you the most time, and you leave with a prioritized list of what is worth building. If a build clearly makes sense, I propose one in writing. If it does not, I tell you.

How much does a build cost?

Every build has a written, fixed price agreed before any paid work starts. The price depends on scope, which we define together after the triage call.

Will I be locked in?

No. You own the code, the data and the accounts. Everything is documented so another developer could take over.

What if AI is not the right fix?

Then I say so on the call. Sometimes a simpler tool, an off-the-shelf product or a change in how the work is done is the better answer.

Can you work with the software we already use?

Usually, yes. Most builds connect existing tools such as email, accounting, CRMs and spreadsheets rather than replacing them.

Who will I actually work with?

Me, Bryce Murad, from the first call to the finished work. I design every system and direct the AI agents that help build and test it.
