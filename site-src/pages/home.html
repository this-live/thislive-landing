Straight answers

Questions owners ask first

What does this.live build?

Custom internal software, workflow automation, AI assistants, websites and private AI setups for small and mid-sized businesses. Most projects connect the tools you already use.

How much does a custom build cost?

The triage call is free. Every build gets a written, fixed price before any work starts, based on what we scope together. You will not get surprise invoices.

How long does a build take?

Your written scope includes a timeline. You see working software every week, not a big reveal at the end.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Explain the work the way you would explain it to a new employee. I handle the technical side and explain decisions in plain language.

Who owns what you build?

You do: the code, the data and the accounts it runs on. If we stop working together, everything stays with you.

Can the AI run on our own computers?

Yes. For sensitive data I can set up AI models that run on hardware you own, so documents never leave your office.

Do you have case studies?

Not yet. The practice is new and I won't invent any. The builds on this page are real, and client results will be published with permission.

Where do you work?

I'm based in Storrs and work with businesses across Connecticut in person or on video, and with clients anywhere else remotely.
