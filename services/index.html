Kinds of work

What a build can look like

  • Following up on new inquiries. Prompt replies and reminders for new leads, so follow-up doesn't depend on someone remembering.
  • Office busywork between systems. The copy-and-paste work between your inbox, spreadsheets and software, and the report someone rebuilds every week.
  • The spreadsheet only one person understands. Turned into a tool the whole team can use, so the business doesn't stall when that person is out.
  • Simple websites and online stores. A clear site or store for your business, set up to work with the tools you already use.
  • AI that runs on your own computer. Local AI models on hardware you own, for work you'd rather not send to an outside service.

Questions and answers

What local owners ask

What does this.live do?

this.live LLC is Bryce Murad's AI consulting practice in Storrs, Connecticut. I help local businesses find the tasks that eat hours every week, then build AI tools and automations that handle them.

Who will I actually work with?

Me, Bryce Murad. You deal with me directly, from the first call to the finished work. Before this.live I was a Network Solutions Architect at Ensono, designing network architecture for 60+ Fortune 1000 clients and state and local governments.

Which areas do you serve?

Tolland County first: Andover, Bolton, Columbia, Coventry, Ellington, Hebron, Mansfield (including Storrs), Somers, Stafford, Tolland, Union, Vernon and Willington. Businesses anywhere else in Connecticut are welcome too.

What happens on the free call?

It's a 30-minute video call. We look at the 1 to 3 workflows that cost you the most time, and you leave with a prioritized list of what's worth automating. If a build clearly makes sense, I'll propose one. If it doesn't, I'll say so.

How much does it cost?

The first 30-minute call is free. Prices for paid packages aren't published yet. Before any paid work starts, you get a written scope and price.

What kinds of tasks can AI take over?

Common examples: following up on new inquiries, moving information between your inbox, spreadsheets and software, the report someone rebuilds every week, a spreadsheet only one person understands, simple websites and online stores, and AI that runs on a computer you own.

Do you have case studies or references?

Not yet. The local consulting practice is new, so there are no client case studies to show, and I won't invent any. You can read about what I build, and how, on the blog.

What happens to my business data?

Before anything is built, I'll tell you which outside services your data would pass through. Where it makes sense, AI can run on hardware you own, so the data stays in your building.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You explain the work the way you'd explain it to a new employee, and I handle the technical side.

How do I get started?

Book a free 30-minute call at cal.com/bryce-murad/automation-triage, or email bryce@this.live.
