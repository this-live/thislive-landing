About

Hi, I'm Bryce Murad.

I help local businesses in Tolland County and across Connecticut put AI to work on the jobs that eat their week.

Why I do this

I'm the founder of this.live LLC, based in Storrs. From 2021 to 2026 I worked at Ensono in Chicago, first as a pre-sales and network intern, then as a Network Solutions Architect designing network architecture for large companies and government agencies.

Today I run this.live with AI. Agents write code, run tests, do research and handle recurring operations; I set the direction and check the work. I bring the same approach to your business: put AI on the tasks it does well, and make sure it's actually doing them.

Outside work I coach swimming at Storrs Aquatics. I swam in college for Southern Connecticut State University, where I earned a B.S. in Sports Management in 2020.

Background

  • Network Solutions Architect, Ensono (Chicago), Feb 2022 to Jan 2026. Designed network architecture for 60+ Fortune 1000 clients and state and local governments.
  • Partnered with Microsoft to design and launch a mainframe-as-a-service product in the public cloud.
  • Built a custom quoting (CPQ) platform that let the company retire a legacy quoting system.
  • Pre-Sales Intern / Network Intern, Ensono, Jun 2021 to Feb 2022.
  • Certifications: Cisco CCNA and Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900). Completed NexGenT's 24-week Full Stack Network Engineer program in 2021.
  • Education: B.S. in Sports Management, Southern Connecticut State University, 2020. NE-10 Conference Champion on the Division II swim team.
  • Lived six years in Singapore and has traveled to more than 30 countries.

Longer versions: résumé and founder page.
