Surfaces: the ways I talk to my AI setup

Surfaces is how I talk to the system: a voice assistant, a daily planner, a terminal and a desktop app, all on one backend I run.

Much of what Surfaces set out to do now lives in Ostium, my newer tool for running and reviewing work across AI coding assistants.

What it does

Pocket Agent

Voice in, work out. I say what I need and it turns into tasks.

Personal Life OS

A daily planner that organizes the day and hands work to the system.

Fleet Terminal

One terminal to drive every machine I run.

Desktop app

All of the above in one window.

Where it stands

Part of my own setup. I'm not selling it as a product today.

What it means for you

The point for clients: your team should talk to its AI in the tools it already uses, not learn six new apps.
