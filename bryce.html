About Bryce Murad

I run this.live. I build custom AI systems and automations for businesses, and I show you how they work as we go, so you end up owning them.

What I do now

I help businesses figure out what's worth automating, build it on the tools they already use, and pick AI tools that don't give their edge away. When the volume or the data justifies it, I move the work onto hardware they control.

I know the tools because I use all of them. I run my own business on a mix of local models on my own machines and frontier models for the hard parts. I track the model releases, test the harnesses, and break things so clients don't have to.

Before this

From 2022 to early 2026 I was a network solutions architect at Ensono in Chicago.

  • Designed network architecture for 60+ Fortune 1000 clients and state and local governments.
  • Technical lead on complex enterprise deals across Fortune 1000 clients and 10+ government agencies, with a better than 50% close rate on deals I supported.
  • Partnered with Microsoft to design and launch a mainframe-as-a-service product in public cloud.
  • Built a custom quoting platform that replaced a legacy system and standardized network products across the company.

That job taught me how to ship complex systems inside real constraints. Things that have to keep working, for people who can't afford for them not to.
