How it works

From a free call to something that runs in your business

Here's what happens, step by step.

The steps

  1. Book a free 30-minute call

    Pick a time on Cal.com. The call is on video. Bring the task or workflow that eats the most hours each week.

  2. Map what's worth automating

    We look at the 1 to 3 workflows that cost you the most time. You leave with a short, prioritized list of opportunities, whether or not you hire me.

  3. Get a written scope and price

    If a build is clearly worth it, I write up what I'll build, what it costs and how we'll both know it works. Discovery is part of the kickoff, not a separate paid audit.

  4. I build it and show it working

    Done means you've seen it do the job in your business, not that it looks finished on my screen.

  5. Keep it running (optional)

    Once something works, I can run and improve it for you on an ongoing basis.

Two common questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. You explain the work the way you'd explain it to a new employee, and I handle the technical side.

What if AI isn't the right fix?

Then I'll say so on the call. Sometimes a simpler tool or a change in how the work is done is the better answer.
