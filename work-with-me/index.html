What I build

Each setup is built for your business, runs in its own private account, and comes with a written guide. You approve anything before it goes out.

Reception agent

Answers website chat, texts, and email: hours, what you sell, directions, and basic questions about what you charge. It takes down the customer's name and what they need, books them on your Google Calendar, and texts you when a person should step in. A phone version is available.

Lead finder and outreach

Watches the public listings that matter to you, like estate sales, permits, new business filings, or job posts, and picks out the good matches. It drafts one honest email per match. You approve it, and replies come to you as leads.

Back-office automation

Form to spreadsheet to invoice or contract. New client intake to a folder and checklist. A weekly numbers report. Built on Google Workspace.
