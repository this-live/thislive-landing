The first thing my marketing agent produced is not allowed to send itself

August 31, 2026

My marketing department produced its first real deliverable this week and it is explicitly forbidden from sending it.

The artifact is a four touch outbound sequence aimed at AI infrastructure founders, written by a campaign manager profile and landed in the Forge vault on 2026-09-04. Its status line says draft only. No sends, no list pulls, no enrolment in any sequencer. It supersedes an earlier version from 2026-08-12, and it names four things that have to be true before a single message goes anywhere: the mail provider has to be connected, the prospect list has to be authorised, the copy and the sender identity have to be approved, and the live Mnemos state document has to be reattached.

Four blockers, named

That list is the part worth keeping. A deliverable that says pending approval tells me nothing. A deliverable that names four specific gates tells me what to do next and, more usefully, tells me which of the four is mine. Two are mine. Two are plumbing.

Draft is a status

I want agents that write outbound. I do not want agents that send outbound, and the difference has to live in the artifact rather than in my intention to be careful. The same week the intake path landed Engineering receipts and the graph engineering canon drafts, including a receipt whose whole purpose was to record that a tree had been committed dirty. Unglamorous, and it is the same principle. Write down the state you are actually in.

The Forge vault is now doing what a vault is for. Marketing and Engineering both put work into it in one week and both left a trail that somebody other than the author can read.

What I am not claiming

I am not going to claim the sequence is good. Nobody has read it against a real prospect, no message has been sent, and a four touch sequence with no reply data is a hypothesis with formatting.

In three months I will tell you whether those four blockers cleared and what the first real replies looked like, including the ones that were nothing.

Thanks for reading. If your automation can send, that capability is a decision you made, whether or not you made it on purpose.
