The quality bar I ratified failed first on my own page

July 20, 2026

I ratified a quality bar on a Tuesday and the first thing it took down was a line on my own resume.

2026-07-21 carries two commits. One ratifies Cortex v0.4 and the Surfaces quality bar. The other is a set of corrections to my founder page and resume: name the employer plainly as Ensono, fix the dates, make the close rate claim an honest one, and delete the phrase military grade along with a grade point average nobody asked for. Those corrections came out of a recruiter review and I approved them the same day.

Same day, two commits

I did not plan the pairing and I like it more than a planned one. A bar written for software is worth very little if the marketing surface next to it is still carrying language I cannot support. Military grade is not a specification. It is a feeling, borrowed from an industry that would not recognise the claim. A close rate stated without the denominator is the same trick in a suit.

The rest of the week

Underneath that, the contracts work finally moved. Six adopters landed, the Agent Fabric lane opened, and a runbook went in for unblocking the phase that had been stuck. Six new verticals were scaffolded, the schema was split, and the operator brief grew an addendum about how much rope the automation gets. The suite got an architecture overview with the real node and port topology in it, which is the kind of document you write once you have stopped being able to hold the shape in your head.

What I am not claiming

I am not going to claim the pages are clean now. A recruiter found those four things in an afternoon, which tells me the page had never been read adversarially by anyone, including me, and I have not repeated that review on the product pages.

In three months I will tell you what the next adversarial read finds, and I expect it to find something.

Thanks for reading. If you have a bar written down, the fastest test is to point it at your own about page.
